SERVICES & OFFICIAL FEES

Available Services & Applicable Fees

Select a service below to view instructions, required documents, and start your online application.

Civil Registration
Birth Certificate

Apply for new birth registration, certificate verification, duplicates, or correction of details on existing birth certificates.

Required Documents: Clinic Card / Notification, Parents' IDs, Proof of Birth.

Fee: TZS 7,000 - 20,000

Civil Registration
Death Certificate

Register a death, request certified copies, or complete verification of death records issued by RITA.

Required Documents: Burial Permit / Hospital Notification, Deceased & Applicant IDs.

Fee: TZS 7,000 - 20,000

Public Online Tool
Track Application Status

Check real-time progress of your submitted application or verify payment status using your application code or GePG Control Number.

Required Numbers: Application Reference Number or GePG Control Number.

Fee: Free

Government Use
Certificate Verification

Verify authenticity of RITA certificates for use in other government services, employment, education, and legal procedures.

Required Information:
• Certificate Number or Application Code
• Applicant Name and Event Year

Fee: 6,000 TZS for Birth and Death Certificates

Re-Issuance
Duplicate Certificate

Apply for a replacement certificate if the original is lost, damaged, or needs re-issuance for official use.

Required Documents:
• Original Certificate Number or Application Record
• Applicant NIDA ID

Fee: 7,000 TZS for Birth and Death Certificates

Record Update
Certificate Correction

Apply for correction of details on a certificate such as names, dates, or other records after submitting legal evidence.

Required Documents:
• Copy of certificate with error
• Proof documents for correct details (e.g: affidavit / hospital letter)

Fee: 13,000 TZS for Birth and Death Certificates

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Get quick answers about using the e-RITA Portal and GePG payments.

Click on the 'Sign In' or 'Register Account' button to log in. Navigate to Birth Certificate Services, select your application type (New, Old to New), upload required documents, and submit.

After submitting your application, a GePG Control Number will be generated. You can pay via M-PESA, TIGO PESA, or NMB / CRDB banks using the provided Control Number.

The average processing time is less than 48 hours once payment and all required documents are verified.
